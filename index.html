Production ML · TAO Trees

TrueClaim

Interpretable decisions. Production infrastructure.

I co-founded TAO Trees and led the architecture and productization of TrueClaim, an insurance fraud decision platform. My work spans proprietary oblique and bivariate trees, training pipelines, counterfactual explanations, real-time scoring and the systems around them.

Python · FastAPI · AWS · Databricks · MLflow

Explore the work +

From data to governed decisions

Designed reproducible ingestion, schema validation and feature engineering for insurance datasets of approximately 2 million claims and 120+ features. Implemented optimized decision trees, imbalanced and cost-sensitive learning, evaluation and rule extraction.

The decision path is the explanation

TrueClaim’s core decision and audit logic is deterministic and model-native. Counterfactuals describe what would change the outcome. LLM narratives can communicate these grounded results to human reviewers.

Engineering the full lifecycle

FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis and RQ support the application plane. AWS provides storage, container workloads, customer isolation and monitoring; Databricks and MLflow support training, model registry, lineage and governance. The lifecycle connects onboarding, training, evaluation, promotion, deployment, monitoring, analyst feedback and retraining.

02 /

Explainable AI · ICAIF 2026

Actionable feature importance

What change would actually change a decision?

Research on exact single-feature counterfactual costs: how far, and at what cost, must a feature change to obtain a target prediction? This makes feature importance actionable for decision trees.

Oblique trees · Counterfactuals · Decision intelligence

Explore the work +

Exact, constrained explanations

The method handles axis-aligned and oblique trees, continuous and categorical features, and user-defined costs and constraints. It can identify when a target outcome is impossible through changing one feature, and help expose model behavior that warrants review.

Research with Miguel Á. Carreira-Perpiñán. Accepted / forthcoming main-track paper at ICAIF 2026 in Milan.

03 /

Scientific ML · PhD research

A digital twin for manufacturing

Physical simulation meets real-time inference.

Developed deep surrogate models and sensor-driven disturbance detection for composite manufacturing. High-fidelity simulations supply synthetic training data; pressure sensors connect the learned models to the physical process.

PyTorch · Encoder-decoder models · Darcy flow

Explore the work +

Simulation → learning → physical validation

Combined disturbance estimation with predictions of pressure and resin flow-front fields. Evaluated the digital twin against physical resin-transfer-moulding experiments, with reported pressure prediction errors below 1% and inference below 50 ms.

04 /

Reinforcement learning · Sim-to-real

Learning to control the physical process

From monitoring to closed-loop control.

Extended manufacturing research into a physics-informed reinforcement learning framework, training a Deep Q-Network in simulation and transferring the controller to laboratory resin-transfer-moulding experiments.

DQN · Sensor feedback · Process control

Explore the work +

Control under physical variability

A Level Set–Darcy environment models changes in fabric permeability and resin viscosity. Using limited pressure-sensor feedback, the trained agent dynamically adjusts inlet pressure to maintain target resin-flow behavior.
